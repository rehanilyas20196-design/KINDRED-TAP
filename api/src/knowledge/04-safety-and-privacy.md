# Safety, support, and privacy

## The support assistant is not a therapist

The assistant built into KINDRED TAP answers questions about the platform. It gives
guidance and wording only.

It does not diagnose, does not treat, does not give medical advice, and does not
respond to a crisis. If it thinks a message is about an emergency rather than a product
question, it says so and points you to real help instead of answering.

## If you need help now

If you might hurt yourself or someone else, do not wait for the platform. Contact your
local emergency number, or a crisis line in your country, or go to the nearest
emergency department.

The assistant is not monitored in real time. Neither are messages sent through the
platform. Nothing here is an emergency service.

## What the assistant will not do

- It will not answer questions about other people's accounts.
- It will not read your messages, sessions, or health records.
- It will not take any action on the platform for you - it cannot book, cancel, refund,
  or change anything.
- It only answers from the documentation the admin team has put in its knowledge base.
  If the answer is not in there, it says it does not know rather than guessing.

## Your data

Your account holds what you typed: your personality test answers, your role profile, and
whatever you choose to write. Professionals involved in your care can see the parts they
need. Admins can see it in order to run the platform and to act on support tickets you
raise.

Matching uses the text described in the matching documentation. It does not use your
messages.

## Who can see what

- A therapy seeker sees their own profile, their matches, and their own sessions.
- A supporter sees what the seeker has chosen to share with them.
- A therapist sees the cases they are working on.
- A clinical advisor or supervisor sees the cases they have been assigned to review.
- A clinic sees the cases belonging to that clinic.

Nobody on the platform can see another person's password. Staff never ask for it.
