# Getting started and account verification

## What happens after you sign up

Every account follows the same first steps:

1. **Personality test.** A short set of questions. The result is used for matching and
   to shape how the platform talks to you. It is not a diagnosis.
2. **Choose your role.** You pick whether you are here as a therapy seeker, a supporter,
   or as a professional (therapist, clinical advisor, clinical supervisor, or
   collaborative clinic).
3. **Complete your role profile.** Each role asks for different information. A therapy
   seeker describes what they are going through; a therapist adds qualifications,
   specialties, and availability.

## Why professionals wait for verification

Therapy seekers and supporters start using the platform straight away.

A professional account waits for an admin to verify it first. This is deliberate: the
people on the platform are being asked to trust these professionals with something
personal, so the credentials get checked by a person before the account is opened.

The admin sees the signup in the verification queue and can approve it or reject it,
with a note explaining why.

## Your verification status

- **Awaiting verification** - you signed up as a professional and the admin has not
  decided yet. You can sign in and see this status, but the professional portal stays
  closed until it changes.
- **Active** - approved. Everything in your portal is available.
- **Rejected** - the admin declined the signup. You can still sign in to read the reason
  given on the rejection screen.
- **Suspended** - the account was closed by an admin after it was active. You cannot use
  the platform while it is suspended.

## What to do if you are still waiting

If it has been more than a day or two, raise a support ticket from your dashboard. The
support team can check where the request is without you having to re-register.

Do not create a second account. Accounts are matched on email address, so a second
account will not speed anything up.
