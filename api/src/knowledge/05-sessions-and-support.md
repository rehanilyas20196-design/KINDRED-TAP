# Sessions, messages, and support tickets

## Sessions

Once you are matched with a therapist you can request a session. Sessions have a time,
a duration, and a mode (video, in person, phone, chat, or async messaging, depending on
what the therapist offers).

The platform blocks a session that would clash with something already on either
calendar, so you will be told at the point of booking rather than after.

Either side can change a session that is already booked - for example to cancel or
reschedule. The other person sees the change.

## Messages

You can message your therapist through the platform. Messaging is not a substitute for
a session and it is not monitored continuously, so it should not be used for anything
urgent.

Messages between partners on a shared case are visible to the people working on that
case, according to the clinic permissions that apply.

## Support tickets

If something is not working, or you have a question the assistant cannot answer, raise a
support ticket from your dashboard.

A ticket is read by the support team. It is the right place to ask about a stuck
verification, a billing problem, or a bug. It is not the right place to ask for clinical
advice.

When you raise a ticket you can add replies, and the team replies on the ticket rather
than by email, so the history stays in one place.

## What to include in a ticket

The page you were on, roughly what time it happened, and what you expected to happen.
That is usually enough to find it. Do not paste anyone else's personal details into a
ticket.
