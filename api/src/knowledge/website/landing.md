# The KINDRED TAP website

## What the platform promises

- Six dedicated portals
- Ethical agreement built in
- Nothing shared without consent

## How someone gets started

**Assessment.** A short, plain-language check-in about how you have been feeling and what you are looking for.
**Describe your situation.** In your own words, without a form full of clinical jargon. Only what you choose to share.
**Match and begin.** We route you to the right kind of support — peer, therapeutic or clinical — and the conversation starts in a private room.

## Principles the platform is built on

**Ethics are part of sign-up.** Supporters, therapists, advisors, supervisors and clinics accept a versioned ethical agreement before they can work.
**Consent before anything moves.** Profiles, notes and matches are only visible to the people taking part in them. Nothing is shared on your behalf.
**Someone is always watching the standard.** Clinical advisors and supervisors review escalations, supervise practitioners and keep the guidelines current.
**Every portal has its own door.** Separate accounts, separate workspaces, separate permissions. Your therapist never sees a clinic's records by accident.

## What the site says

- Six portals, one per role. People seeking support, peer supporters, therapists, clinical advisors, supervisors and partner clinics each sign in to their own workspace — so the right people see the right things, and nobody else does.
- The path to support: a short assessment, a description in your own words, then a match and a private room — nothing leaves your account without consent.
