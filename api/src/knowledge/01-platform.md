# What KINDRED TAP is

KINDRED TAP is an online therapy platform. It helps a person looking for support find a
suitable therapist, and gives the professionals around that person the tools to work
together on the same case.

It is an online platform. It is not a hospital, an emergency service, or a replacement
for in-person care.

## Who uses it

There are six kinds of account:

- **Therapy seeker** - someone looking for support. They complete the personality test,
  describe what they are going through, and get matched with therapists.
- **Supporter** - a friend, partner, or family member who is supporting someone through
  therapy. Supporters can follow along and use the supporter tools.
- **Therapist** - a practitioner who offers sessions on the platform.
- **Clinical advisor** - a senior clinician who reviews cases and advises.
- **Clinical supervisor** - a supervisor who oversees therapist practice on the platform.
- **Collaborative clinic** - a clinic that brings several professionals together on shared
  cases.

## What it is not

KINDRED TAP does not give medical advice, does not diagnose, and does not prescribe.
Nothing on the platform is a substitute for seeing a doctor or a mental health
professional in person.

If you think you are in danger, or someone else is, contact your local emergency number
now.
