# AI matching

## How matching works

When a therapy seeker completes their profile, KINDRED TAP turns what they have written
into a text summary of what they are looking for: their stated concern, the result of
their assessment, and their own description in their own words.

Each therapist profile is turned into a text summary the same way, from their
specialties, the modes they work in, and what they have written about their practice.

Both summaries are converted into numbers (embeddings) that represent meaning rather
than keywords. A therapist is ranked by how close their numbers are to the seeker's.
The ranking is a similarity score, nothing more. It is not a clinical judgement and it
is not a prediction of outcome.

## Consent is required

AI matching only runs for therapists who have turned on consent to AI matching in their
profile, and only for seekers who have described their concern. You can change your
consent setting in your therapist profile at any time.

## What the matching engine reads

For a therapist: specialties, session modes, languages, and the text they wrote about
themselves.

For a seeker: their current concern, their assessment result, and their own description.

It does not read your messages, your sessions, your payment details, or anything else.

## Getting better matches

The single most useful thing a seeker can do is write a real description of what they
want help with, rather than a single word. "Panic attacks that started after I changed
job, I want tools for the physical symptoms" gives the engine far more to work with
than "anxiety".

For therapists, filling in every specialty and writing a genuine bio matters more than
any keyword. The engine reads meaning, so writing naturally works better than trying to
guess what the system wants.

## What matching is not

It is not a diagnosis, not a recommendation of a specific person, and not a guarantee
that any therapist is right for you. You are free to ignore the ranking entirely and
browse therapists yourself.
